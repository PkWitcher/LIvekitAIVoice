1. Getting Started

1.1Landing Page

Visit your Nova AI web address to reach the public landing page. This page introduces the platform and gives you a single entry point — the Get Started button — to reach the sign-in screen.

1.2Creating an Account

Click Get Started to open the login screen, then switch to the sign-up form.

  1. Click Get Started on the landing page (or go directly to the Login screen).
  2. Select Create your account.
  3. Enter your Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address and a Password.
  4. Submit the form. A confirmation email is sent to your inbox.
  5. Click the confirmation link in the email, then return to the Login screen to sign in.

3. Making a Single Call

Open the Calls tab in the sidebar to reach the Call Dispatcher — used to place one AI-powered call at a time.

  1. Enter the recipient's phone number (with country code, e.g. +91 98765 43210).
  2. Write what the AI agent should say/discuss in the prompt box — or describe your goal in the brief field and click Generate to have AI write the call script for you.
  3. Choose a voice and language for the agent.
  4. Optionally click the star icon to save a prompt for reuse, or open Saved Prompts to load one you saved earlier.
  5. Click Dispatch Call to place the call immediately.
  6. Once connected, click View Live Transcript to watch the conversation unfold in real time.
AI Prompt Generator: Don't want to write a script from scratch? Type a short brief (e.g. "Remind the customer about their appointment tomorrow at 5 PM") and Nova AI drafts a ready-to-use conversation prompt for you.

4. Bulk Calling Campaigns

Below the single Call Dispatcher on the Calls screen is the Bulk Dialer — for running the same script across many contacts in one go.

  1. Paste a list of phone numbers, one per line (or comma-separated) into the numbers box.
  2. Write or AI-generate the shared call context/prompt that every call will use.
  3. Review the automatically detected contact count.
  4. Click Launch Campaign to dispatch calls to every number in the list.
  5. Each number's dispatch result (dispatched / failed) appears in a results list below.
Note: Bulk campaigns consume your plan's call and minute quota just like single calls — check your Subscription card before launching a large batch.

5. Call History

Also on the Calls screen, Call History lists every call your account has made — phone number, date/time, duration, direction (inbound/outbound) and final status (Completed, No Answer, Failed, Ringing). Use it to audit past campaigns or follow up on unanswered calls.

6. Transcripts

Open the Transcripts tab in the sidebar to review the full text of past conversations between the AI agent and your customers. Select any call to read the turn-by-turn dialogue, useful for quality review or verifying what was promised on a call.

8. AI Analytics Assistant

A floating chat assistant is available on every dashboard screen (bottom-right corner). Click it to ask plain-English questions about your call data — for example:

"How many calls did I complete last week?" · "What's my pickup rate this month?" · "Which numbers haven't answered yet?"

The assistant reads your account's live call data and answers instantly, without needing to build a report manually.
